Tamil Business Tribe (TBT) is the official mobile app for members of the Tamil Business Tribe community — a business-education platform that combines structured learning, live workshops, batch programs, and peer accountability.

WHAT YOU CAN DO IN THE APP
• Watch on-demand courses with progress tracking, quizzes, and completion certificates
• Join workshops with instructor Q&A, submitted assignments, and live sessions
• Follow a day-by-day batch program with task submission, attendance, and XP-based progress
• Track your learning: continue-watching, watch history, badges, and leaderboards
• Chat and message with your batch cohort and support team
• Access downloadable resources gated to your batch
• Get real-time push notifications for approvals, live-call reminders, and announcements

BUILT FOR MEMBERS
This app is for existing TBT members and members-in-registration. New sign-ups require phone-number verification via WhatsApp OTP. Access to premium content depends on your membership tier.

TECHNOLOGY
TBT is built with Flutter for a fast, native experience on both Android and iOS. Videos stream in HLS with adaptive bitrate. All content is delivered from our servers in India for low-latency playback.

WHO WE ARE
Tamil Business Tribe is a business education initiative dedicated to helping Tamil-speaking entrepreneurs grow through structured learning, mentorship, and community.
